Luma 1.0 RC1

Welcome to Luma.

Luma is a native music player for your Navidrome and OpenSubsonic libraries, designed for a calm, focused listening experience.

HIGHLIGHTS
• Synchronized lyrics that follow along as you listen.
• Lossless audio and gapless playback for uninterrupted albums.
• A beautiful library for browsing artists, albums, songs, and playlists.
• Full-screen artwork, fluid animations, and a player designed for iPhone and iPad.
• Lock Screen and Control Center playback controls.
• Playlist editing with artwork, reordering, and track management.
• Offline downloads for listening without a network connection.
• Native support for iOS and macOS, with widgets and system integrations.

PRIVACY
• No analytics, telemetry, or Luma account.
• Your music library stays with the server you configure.

PLEASE TEST
• Sign in to a Navidrome or OpenSubsonic server and browse your library.
• Play an album from beginning to end with repeat off.
• Check synchronized lyrics, seeking, Lock Screen controls, and volume.
• Edit a playlist, download music, then try playback while offline.
• Try the app on both iPhone and iPad if available.
