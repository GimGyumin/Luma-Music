Luma Music 1.0 RC2 (1031)

WHAT'S NEW
• Expanded widgets: Now Playing Large, Pinned Small, Recent and Recommendations, with playback actions on supported widgets.
• Siri AI / App Intents: more library, playback, queue, playlist and search actions on supported iOS 27.3+ devices.
• Navidrome Replay: server-backed listening history, offline sync queue and improved Listen Again / Wrapped recommendations.
• Smarter streaming cache maintenance, including safeguards for actively playing and downloaded files.
• Optional, consent-based Firebase crash and performance diagnostics, plus contextual help guides.

FIXED & IMPROVED
• Playback recovery, gapless transitions, end-of-queue handling and saved playback position.
• AAC stream handling, corrupt-cache retry loops and cache-file lifetime during playback.
• Queue selection, Songs sorting/play order and favorite indicators.
• Album navigation/tab bar stability and reduced SwiftUI/SwiftData stalls.

PLEASE TEST
• Stream AAC/lossless music; skip tracks, seek, pause for a while and resume. Check gapless transitions and the end of an album.
• Force-close and reopen the app; confirm the last track/position and that a new playback command is not overwritten.
• Reorder a queue and play from sorted Songs; verify the intended song starts.
• Try widgets, light/dark mode, slow-network loading, album swipe-back transitions and offline downloads.
• Check Navidrome Replay sync and optional diagnostics with sharing on/off. Do not include private server addresses, tokens or account data in feedback.
• On compatible iOS 27.3+ devices, test Siri AI actions and report any failed commands.

This is an RC build. Please include your device, OS, build 1031 and reproduction steps when reporting bugs.
