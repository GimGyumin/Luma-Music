## Art that takes the stage.

Your album covers shape Now Playing, the album page, and supported Lock Screen experiences. Some covers and devices **do not support immersive presentation**.

## Lyrics that move with the song.

Luma can show line-synchronized lyrics and **TTML word-timed highlighting** when the server or configured source provides compatible data. Plain lyric text alone doesn't provide word timings.

## Translation and pronunciation.

Optional Apple/OpenAI-powered lyric translation can work with available lyric text, where supported and configured.

**Pronunciation and romanization require a compatible pronunciation layer in the supplied lyric data.** They are not generated for every track.

## Sound that stays connected.

Enjoy **gapless playback** with supported albums, lossless audio where the source and playback route allow it, crossfade, ReplayGain, queue controls, and offline downloads.

## Your library, everywhere.

Library browsing, local search, supported Siri/Shortcuts actions, Spotlight, widgets, and system playback controls help make Luma feel at home on iPhone and iPad. Some system integrations depend on OS, hardware, and signing entitlements.

Accessibility includes VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion, and English/Korean localization.
