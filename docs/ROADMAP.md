## Now. Making it feel right.

The app and this GitHub repository are still being refined. We're focusing on playback stability, smooth interactions, consistent design, and clearer documentation.

## November 2026. RC3: player, lyrics, and iPad improvements.

RC3 addresses the previously reported iPad interface issues and substantially updates the player, lyric backgrounds, adaptive lyric scrolling, and English-to-Korean pronunciation fallback. **iPad users should install RC3 once its signed IPA is published.** Until then, RC2 remains the currently available build and does not include these fixes. See the [RC3 preview notes](releases/v1.0.0-rc3.md).

## Next. Validate RC3 and keep refining.

**Release candidates will continue** while we work toward stable 1.0. Priorities include release-build verification, real-device testing, data retention, and any remaining regressions. Timing depends on readiness.

## Early 2027. Luma Music 1.0.

We no longer expect to meet our original **end-of-2026** launch target. The first stable release of **Luma Music 1.0 for iPhone and iPad** is now **targeted for early 2027**, subject to final testing and readiness. We appreciate your patience as we prioritize stability and the iPad experience.

## Next. Easier to understand.

| Planned improvement | What to expect |
| --- | --- |
| **Feature documentation** | Clear guides explaining how supported features work and what data they need. |
| **Server setup walkthrough** | Beginner-friendly Navidrome configuration and connection instructions. |
| **Simpler onboarding** | More approachable IPA installation and troubleshooting documentation. |

A basic [installation guide](GETTING_STARTED.md) and [feature overview](FEATURES.md) are available now. More detailed tutorials are planned.

## 2027. On Mac and Apple Watch.

We're targeting 2027 for dedicated Luma Music apps for **Mac (macOS)** and **Apple Watch (watchOS)**. We plan to make beta builds of both apps available through **TestFlight in 2027**.
