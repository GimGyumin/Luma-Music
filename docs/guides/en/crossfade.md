# About Crossfade

Crossfade overlaps the end of the current track with the start of the next one.

## Settings

- **Duration**: how long the fade lasts. Very long fades can overlap vocals.
- **Automatic**: begins only near the end of the playing track.
- **Not with Gapless**: when Crossfade is enabled it replaces the gapless handoff.

## Notes

- Gapless may suit live albums and classical music better.
- The next source is prepared in advance; a slow network can delay the start.
- Seeking or changing tracks during a fade cancels it and starts the new position.
