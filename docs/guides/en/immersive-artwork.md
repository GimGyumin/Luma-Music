# About Immersive Artwork

Immersive artwork extends an album cover's color and composition into the album detail page.

## When it applies

- The server cover must load at a sufficient resolution.
- Covers with stable edge color and texture extend most naturally.
- Transparent PNGs, very complex collages, and low-resolution images may use a standard card.
- Device performance and accessibility settings can delay preparation.

## Notes

The choice is automatic per album. A cover that cannot extend still plays and downloads normally. Turning off Immersive Album Artwork in Settings shows every album as a standard card.
