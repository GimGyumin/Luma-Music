# About Lossless Audio

Luma Music plays the original file your server provides without converting it to a lossy format. Depending on the selected quality and the server file, FLAC, ALAC, and high-resolution PCM are delivered as-is.

## Things to know

- Lossless files are larger and may take longer to produce the first sound.
- Bluetooth headphones and some output devices may re-compress the audio.
- When the server offers a transcoded stream, the player badge shows the format actually delivered.
- Downloaded originals play offline at their original quality.

## If startup is slow

1. Retry on Wi-Fi.
2. Check the server's upload speed and concurrent sessions.
3. Leave quality on Automatic so Luma can adapt to the connection.

Choosing Lossless does not turn a lossy source into lossless audio.
