# About Personalized Recommendations

The Playlists tab shows Luma recommendations derived from your listening history in a section separate from server playlists.

## Listen Again

- Only albums with at least two distinct tracks played in the last 120 days are candidates.
- Repeating one song is not treated as listening to the whole album.
- Albums with a completed track or substantial listening are ranked by recency.
- If history is insufficient, no synthetic album is created and the section is hidden.
- Selecting a card opens the real album detail.

## Luma mixes

Get Up!, Heavy Rotation, Your Essentials, New Music, and the other Luma mixes are computed on-device, not server playlists. They stay above user playlists regardless of the server's sort order, and empty mixes are omitted.

Recommendations reuse existing listening and library metadata without extra server requests.
