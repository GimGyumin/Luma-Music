# Using Luma Widgets

Luma offers Now Playing, Recently Played, Made for You, and Pins widgets.

## Adding a widget

1. Touch and hold the Home Screen.
2. Choose **Edit → Add Widget**.
3. Find Luma and select a size.

Small, Medium, and Large use different information density. Recently Played shows 1, 3, or 7 tracks; Made for You shows up to six Luma mixes.

## Refresh behavior

- Changing tracks refreshes Now Playing and Recently Played.
- Newly computed recommendation mixes refresh the Made for You widget.
- Otherwise iOS refreshes widgets on its timeline schedule.
- A saved snapshot may remain visible after the app is terminated.

## If a widget is missing or stale

Open Luma once so the shared widget snapshot is prepared, then add the widget again. Immediately after an app update, iOS may briefly cache the old widget gallery metadata.
