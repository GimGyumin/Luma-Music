**Second release candidate · iPhone and iPad IPA preview**

*Your music. More ways to make it yours.*

RC2 brings deeper Siri AI integration, more useful widgets, and a more personal Home experience. It also improves playback startup, navigation, storage management, and the everyday details that make listening feel effortless.

## Download and installation

1. Install it using a compatible IPA signing and sideloading method, such as SideStore or AltStore, on a supported iPhone or iPad.
2. Open Luma Music and connect to your own compatible music server.

This is a **pre-release**, not the final 1.0 release. Luma is not currently distributed through the App Store or TestFlight.

## What's new in RC2

### More you can do with Siri AI

Siri can work with more of your music library through an expanded set of **App Intents and 12 AI tools**. Supported actions include searching by genre, year, and file format; editing the playback queue; accessing favorites; managing playlists; working with available lyrics; and viewing or changing supported app settings. Availability depends on the device, system capabilities, and content.

### More personal widgets

**Recent** and **Recommendations** join Now Playing and Pins for a total of **four widget types**. Supported widgets now include additional sizes, playback controls, and artwork-inspired backgrounds.

### Your music, ready sooner

Personalized mixes now appear as they become available instead of waiting for the entire set to load. **Listen Again** uses actual listening activity to surface one album on Home.

### Smoother navigation and playback

Album and playlist navigation is more consistent, including interactive back gestures and tab bar visibility. Startup work is better coordinated so lyric loading and listening-history synchronization do not compete with the first audio output.

### Smarter storage management

Streaming cache can now be cleaned up automatically using access history, age, and available storage. Permanent downloads and actively playing tracks are protected.

### Clearer settings and help

Advanced settings have been consolidated. Contextual guides explain features such as lyrics, audio quality, downloads, Siri, and widgets. Optional diagnostics operate with user consent.

- **Siri AI:** 23 App Intents, 12 AI tools, and 5 entities, with English and Korean definitions. Expanded queue, favorites, playlist, lyric, search, and settings actions; App Skills bundle included.

## Important notes

- Luma requires your own separately configured, compatible Navidrome, OpenSubsonic, or supported Jellyfin music server. It does not include a commercial music catalog.
- Audio format support, lossless playback, lyrics, and immersive artwork depend on available source content and compatible devices.
- Siri AI capabilities require compatible system features and may vary with permissions, signing, and device configuration. Static analyzer results do not guarantee every spoken request works.
- RC2 remains a release candidate. Some interactive swipe transitions, widget layouts, accessibility cases, and Siri interactions need further real-device verification.

For installation details and project status, see the [README](https://github.com/GimGyumin/Luma-Music).

Report bugs and ideas in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GimGyumin/Luma-Music/issues). Feedback about playback stability, lyric synchronization, widgets, and Siri is especially helpful.
